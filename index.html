The singer is a real neural voice model (Kokoro, open source and free) that runs right on your device. No account or internet service is needed. It sings your words on the melody with natural slides, vibrato and breath. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 90 MB, Wi-Fi recommended). After that it's saved and works offline. The vocals record in the background while the song plays, and every voice, style and lyric change is in the WAV download.
